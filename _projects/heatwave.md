As editor and developer, I led **data sourcing**, **visual narrative planning**, and **independent web development**, delivering a journalism piece that merged evidence-based reporting with visual impact.

- Built a mobile-first interactive story experience with visual maps and quotes
- Blended data journalism with social advocacy, highlighting **climate injustice**
