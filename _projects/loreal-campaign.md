<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">
Led a team to design an integrated campaign combining **short films, podcasts, art therapy workshops**, and **AI-assisted co-creation tools** to reshape beauty narratives among Gen Z.
As project lead, I conducted early-stage user research and developed the core **creative direction**, emphasizing emotional authenticity and inclusive expression.

- Projected to reach **2M+ students** and generate **5M+ impressions**
- Strengthened L'Oréal's **Gen Z engagement strategy** through multi-channel storytelling
</div>

<div data-lang="zh" markdown="1">
带队做的一套整合传播方案，把短片、播客、艺术疗愈工作坊和 AI 辅助共创工具组合起来，去松动 Z 世代关于"美"的既有叙事。我是项目负责人，做了前期用户研究，定下核心创意方向，重点放在情绪的真实性和表达的包容度上。

- 预计触达200万以上学生，产生500万以上曝光
- 以多渠道叙事强化欧莱雅面向 Z 世代的沟通策略
</div>
