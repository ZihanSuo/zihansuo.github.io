<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">
Analyzed brand positioning, influencer strategy, and platform differentiation in the Chinese fitness market.
Compared **WeChat**, **Xiaohongshu**, and **Mini Programs** to evaluate how each brand builds its digital identity and community presence.

- Highlighted lululemon's emotional branding vs. Decathlon's accessibility model
- Assessed localization tactics and ambassador resonance
</div>

<div data-lang="zh" markdown="1">
分析中国健身市场里的品牌定位、达人策略和平台差异化，把微信、小红书和小程序三个场域放在一起比，看两个品牌各自怎么建立自己的数字身份和社群存在感。

- lululemon 走情绪品牌，迪卡侬走可及性，两条路的底层逻辑差别很大
- 评估了各自的本地化打法，以及品牌大使实际产生的共鸣程度
