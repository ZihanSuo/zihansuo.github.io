<div class="stage">
<h2><span data-lang="en">Why we built it</span><span data-lang="zh">起因</span></h2>

<div data-lang="en">
<p>Head & Shoulders was launching a foam shampoo, dispensed as foam rather than liquid. The brief asked for Douyin short video that would drive trade-in purchase through talkability, with a hard ordering of benefits (oil control and volume first, dandruff second), a total budget of 50,000 to 100,000 RMB, and success measured in direct sales and ROI.</p>
<p>The brief framed the product around speed: one press to foam, no lathering, done faster.</p>
</div>

<div data-lang="zh">
<p>海飞丝推出泡沫洗发水，挤出来直接是泡沫而非液体。命题要求在抖音用短视频制造话题、带动购买，并给出了硬性约束：功效排序为控油蓬松第一、去屑第二，总预算5至10万元，考核以直接销量与 ROI 为准。</p>
<p>brief 对产品的定位是"快"：一按出泡，省去搓泡，洗得更快。</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="stage">
<h2><span data-lang="en">Our approach</span><span data-lang="zh">思路</span></h2>

<div data-lang="en">
<p><strong>Locate the gap in the data before choosing an audience.</strong> On Douyin, 24 to 30 year olds are the largest share of shampoo viewers at 31.92% but only second in purchases at 25.40%. For Head & Shoulders specifically, 30.11% of viewers are 24 to 30 while 46.05% of buyers are 31 to 40. Young users watch and do not buy. The campaign targets that break, with a broad 18 to 44 base for reach and a precise 24 to 30, female-led audience for the ad itself.</p>
<p><strong>Treat workarounds as evidence of unmet need.</strong> Foaming nets, volume powder, dry shampoo spray, washing only the fringe, prepaid salon wash cards, hats. People already pay in money and effort to avoid washing their hair properly. That behaviour is the demand signal, and it is stronger than anything a survey would return.</p>
<p><strong>Test whether "fast" is the real selling point.</strong> Every benefit on the list was plausible and the product could claim all of them, so the question was which one people would pay more for. Foam shampoo had no comment base yet, so the team crawled 1,200 comments under Safeguard's foam body wash on Douyin as the closest analogue.</p>
</div>

<div data-lang="zh">
<p><strong>先在数据里找断层，再定人群。</strong>抖音洗发水品类中，24至30岁观众占比第一（31.92%），成交占比却只排第二（25.40%）。具体到海飞丝，24至30岁占观众的30.11%，而成交主力是31至40岁（46.05%）。年轻用户只看不买。传播对准这道断层：以18至44岁泛人群保证覆盖，广告本身则聚焦24至30岁、以女性为主的精准人群。</p>
<p><strong>把补偿行为当作未满足需求的证据。</strong>起泡网、蓬蓬粉、免洗喷雾、只洗刘海、办洗头卡、戴帽子遮挡。消费者已经在用钱和精力回避好好洗头，这种行为本身就是需求信号，比问卷能给出的任何答案都强。</p>
<p><strong>检验"快"是不是真卖点。</strong>功效清单上每一项都成立，产品也都能满足，问题在于哪一项能让人多付钱。泡沫洗发水当时还没有评论积累，团队以最接近的品类作参照，爬取抖音舒肤佳泡沫沐浴露下的1200条评论。</p>
</div>

<div class="callout">
  <p class="label"><span data-lang="en">What the comments said</span><span data-lang="zh">评论给出的答案</span></p>
  <p><span data-lang="en">People did not talk about time. They talked about foam and scent, which is to say about how it felt. <strong>Nobody is actually short of two minutes. Washing your hair is a chore, and the chore feels bad.</strong> Speed is a supporting benefit. The real one is turning the task into something that feels good, and keeping you looking good afterwards.</span><span data-lang="zh">评论里没有人在谈时间，谈的是泡沫和香味，也就是体感。<strong>没有人真的缺那两分钟。洗头是一项任务，而这项任务的体感很差。</strong>"快"是辅助价值，真正的价值是把任务变成体感好的事，并且洗完之后形象好。</span></p>
</div>

<div data-lang="en">
<p>That gave the campaign its line: hard power for a good look, soft power for a good feel. It also kept the brief's benefit ordering intact, since oil control and volume are what "looking good" means here, and dandruff sits beneath it.</p>
<p><strong>Script the insight, not the product.</strong> A 38-second 9:16 spot built on two everyday scenes. It opens on a woman on a sofa at 8:30 pm talking herself out of washing her hair, the clock jumping to 11. The product arrives as relief rather than as a feature list. A morning scene uses a three-minute microwave timer as the clock the fringe wash has to beat.</p>
</div>

<div data-lang="zh">
<p>由此得出传播主张：硬实力给你好形象，软实力给你好体感。它也保住了 brief 的功效排序，因为这里的"好形象"指的正是控油蓬松，去屑居其次。</p>
<p><strong>把洞察写进剧本，而不是把产品写进剧本。</strong>一支38秒的9:16竖屏广告，两个生活化场景。开场是晚上八点半，女生躺在沙发上反复说服自己不洗头，时钟跳到十一点。产品以解脱的方式出现，而不是一张功效清单。早晨那场戏用微波炉的三分钟计时，作为洗刘海要赶上的时钟。</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="stage">
<h2><span data-lang="en">Postmortem</span><span data-lang="zh">事后复盘</span></h2>

<div data-lang="en">
<p><strong>It was never run, and the deck makes that easy to miss.</strong> The projected seven days after launch (5,377 units, 424,000 RMB, 10% conversion) is laid out in the same table format as the real seven days before it (100 to 250 units, 10,000 to 25,000 RMB). Side by side, a projection reads like a result. It should have been labelled as one.</p>
<p><strong>The total is over the brief's ceiling.</strong> Production at 46,700 and distribution at 59,500 are presented in separate tables, and each fits under 100,000 on its own. The deck's own ROAS formula adds them, to 106,200. That is not an accounting ambiguity; the deck resolves it against itself.</p>
<p><strong>ROAS uses gross projected revenue, not the increment.</strong> The baseline week already sold 10,000 to 25,000 RMB. Measured on the uplift, the figure falls to roughly 3.8. Small, but it is the number the brief said it would judge on.</p>
<p><strong>The jump is not modelled.</strong> Going from 100 to 250 units a week to 5,377 is a twenty- to fifty-fold increase attributed to one 38-second spot and 59,500 RMB of distribution. Nothing in the deck derives it from reach, click-through and conversion rates, even though the measurement framework lists all three.</p>
<p><strong>The central insight rests on an analogue.</strong> The 1,200 comments are about foam body wash, not foam shampoo. Transferring "people care about feel, not time" from one to the other is reasonable and still an assumption. The fringe-washing use case, which is specific to shampoo, is exactly where the two might differ.</p>
<p><strong>One production risk nobody priced.</strong> The bathroom scene sets new lyrics to the melody of an existing hit song. In a paid commercial that needs a licence, and the budget has no line for music.</p>
</div>

<div data-lang="zh">
<p><strong>它从未执行，而方案的排版让这一点容易被忽略。</strong>广告上线后7天的预测数据（销量5377、销售额42.4万、转化率10%），与上线前真实的7天数据（销量100至250、销售额1万至2.5万）用同一种表格并排呈现。放在一起，预测读起来像结果。它本应被明确标注为预测。</p>
<p><strong>总费用超出了 brief 的上限。</strong>制作费4.67万与投放费5.95万分列两张表，单看都在10万以内。而方案自己的 ROAS 公式把二者相加，合计10.62万。这不是口径含糊，方案自己给出了答案。</p>
<p><strong>ROAS 用的是预测总收入，不是增量。</strong>基线周本身已有1万至2.5万的销售额。按增量计算，ROAS 约降至3.8。差距不大，但这正是 brief 说要据以评判的那个数字。</p>
<p><strong>销量跃升没有建模。</strong>从每周100至250件到5377件，是20至50倍的增长，归因于一支38秒广告和5.95万投放。方案没有从曝光、点击率、转化率推导出这个数字，尽管评估框架里三项都列了。</p>
<p><strong>核心洞察建立在参照品类上。</strong>1200条评论来自泡沫沐浴露，不是泡沫洗发水。把"消费者在意体感而非时间"从前者迁移到后者是合理的，但仍是假设。洗刘海这个洗发水特有的场景，恰恰是两者可能不同的地方。</p>
<p><strong>一项没被计价的制作风险。</strong>浴室那场戏用一首现成流行歌的旋律改写了歌词。商业广告使用需要授权，而预算里没有音乐这一项。</p>
</div>
